Do not call

⚠ This list is yours only. It is not the National Do Not Call Registry — scrubbing against that is a separate subscription (telemarketing.donotcall.gov) and is legally required before calling anyone who did not opt in.

Settings

Stored locally in SQLite. Nothing is sent anywhere but Twilio and Anthropic.

Twilio

Sign up at twilio.com/try-twilio, buy a voice-capable number (about $1.15/month), and paste the credentials from the console here.

Caller identity

Prepended and appended to every script automatically. A pre-recorded marketing call must identify who is calling and offer a way out.

Calling rules

Local time where the recipient lives, from their area code.
Federal limit is 8am–9pm; several states are stricter.
Pacing. Blasting a list gets the number flagged as spam.

AI script writer

Powers the "Draft with AI" button. Everything else works without it.

Delivery mode

Blank = direct mode: the script travels with the call and nothing needs to be reachable from the internet. Set it (e.g. an ngrok tunnel) for webhook mode, which adds press-9-to-opt-out, hanging up on voicemail, and live status callbacks.
